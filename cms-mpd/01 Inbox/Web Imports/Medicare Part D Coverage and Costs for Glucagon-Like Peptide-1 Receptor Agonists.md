> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- This cross-sectional study examined 2020 to 2025 Part D formulary files for GLP-1 receptor agonists.
- Coverage of most GLP-1s for diabetes remained broad, with 2025 beneficiary-weighted coverage rates generally above 85 percent.
- Coverage for nondiabetes indications remained negligible in 2025, with no beneficiary coverage for tirzepatide for obstructive sleep apnea and only 1.0 percent coverage for injectable semaglutide for cardiovascular disease.
- Prior authorization for GLP-1s was uncommon through 2023 but became nearly universal in 2025.
- Out-of-pocket costs rose sharply in 2025 as plans increasingly shifted to coinsurance, with the increase concentrated in stand-alone PDPs.
