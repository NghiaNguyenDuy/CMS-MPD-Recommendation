> [!note]
> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- OIG summarized 15 years of annual analyses on Part D formulary coverage for the top 200 drugs used by dual-eligible enrollees.
- It found that most Part D plan formularies have covered almost all of the drugs dual-eligible enrollees commonly use since 2011, with consistently high coverage over the last decade.
- OIG also found that the number of commonly used drugs covered by all formularies steadily increased since 2011.
- The agency concluded that, in general, dual-eligible enrollees had and continue to have access to the drugs they most commonly use regardless of plan enrollment.
