> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- KFF analyzed CMS Medicare Part D claims data from 2019 through 2024.
- Ozempic was used by 2 million Part D enrollees in 2024, up from fewer than 150,000 in 2019.
- Gross Medicare Part D spending on GLP-1s reached $27.5 billion in 2024, a five-fold increase from 2019.
- KFF notes that if rebates were around 50 percent, net spending would still be around $14 billion in 2024.
- GLP-1 claims rose from 4.8 million in 2019 to 21.8 million in 2024.
