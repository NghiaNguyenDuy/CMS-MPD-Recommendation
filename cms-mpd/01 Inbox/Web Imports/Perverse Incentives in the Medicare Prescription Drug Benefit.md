> [!note]
> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- The paper argues that risk adjustment in a stand-alone Medicare drug benefit can create perverse incentives for plan coverage decisions and pharmaceutical pricing.
- It says this problem is more severe than in other forms of health coverage because drug makers with patented products have market power and plans can target very specific patient groups through formulary design.
- The authors argue that the drug benefit therefore needs unusually close regulatory oversight.
- They also suggest that requiring all plans to charge the same premium would reduce quality diversity but could lower budget uncertainty and reduce upward pressure on drug prices.
