- KFF describes a two-step CMS approach: the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge from July 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026, followed by the BALANCE Model beginning in January 2027 if participation thresholds are met.
- Participation in BALANCE is voluntary for drug manufacturers, state Medicaid programs, and Medicare Part D plans.
- CMS set the Medicare plan application date as April 20, 2026 and will decide by April 30, 2026 whether the model reaches the required 80 percent participation threshold.
- Participating Part D plans in 2027 must place all model drugs on the same formulary tier, cover all medically accepted indications, and apply the same cost sharing to all indications.
- KFF notes that BALANCE could lower out-of-pocket costs for some beneficiaries but may also raise premiums or disrupt coverage if plans do not participate or later leave the model.
