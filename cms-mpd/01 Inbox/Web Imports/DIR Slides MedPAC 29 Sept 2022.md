- The presentation analyzes 2020 direct and indirect remuneration data for 30 brand-name drugs across 10 categories.
- Average rebates ranged from under 10 percent for antineoplastics to at least 50 percent for diabetic therapies.
- The slides argue that organizational differences across sponsors, PBMs, contracts, and formularies may contribute to substantial DIR variation.
