> [!note]
> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- This study uses CMS public use files to examine whether Part D plans used co-payments or co-insurance for preferred branded drugs from 2020 to 2024.
- It finds that stand-alone PDPs increasingly used co-insurance rather than flat co-payments for tier 3 preferred brand drugs, while MA-PDs mostly kept co-payments.
- For apixaban, the study reports that mean out-of-pocket costs in PDPs more than doubled from 2020 to 2024, while MA-PD out-of-pocket costs stayed comparatively flat.
- The authors argue that co-insurance exposes beneficiaries to rising list prices while rebates accrue to plans rather than being shared at the point of sale.
