> [!note]
> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- Part D covered more than 51 million Medicare beneficiaries in 2023.
- MedPAC reported 2022 Part D expenditures of $117.3 billion.
- The report says Part D's LIS supported nearly 14 million beneficiaries and that Medicare spent $39.7 billion on LIS in 2022.
- MedPAC highlights that generics expanded over time, but a relatively small share of biologics and specialty medicines accounted for a growing share of spending.
- The report says cost-based reinsurance had become a growing share of program spending and weakened plan incentives to control costs.
