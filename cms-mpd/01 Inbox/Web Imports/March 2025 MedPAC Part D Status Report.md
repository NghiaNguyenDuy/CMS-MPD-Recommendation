> [!note]
> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- Part D covered more than 54 million Medicare beneficiaries in 2024.
- For 2023, MedPAC reported total Part D payments of $128.2 billion, including $68.2 billion for basic-benefit subsidies, $43.9 billion in LIS support, and $16.1 billion in basic-benefit premiums.
- MedPAC says the 2025 redesign includes key elements of its earlier recommendations to restore plan incentives to manage spending.
- The report says that in recent years more than 90 percent of Medicare's basic-benefit payments had taken the form of reinsurance rather than capitated direct subsidy payments.
- MedPAC identifies single-source brand-name drugs and biologics as major drivers of overall Part D price growth.
