> [!note]
> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- Part D covered more than 55 million Medicare beneficiaries in 2025.
- MedPAC estimated that Part D spending grew nearly 18 percent between 2023 and 2024.
- The report says 2024 payments totaled $148.3 billion, including $90.3 billion for basic-benefit subsidies, $41.3 billion for LIS support, $16.7 billion in enrollee premiums for basic benefits, $17.7 billion in cost sharing, and $0.5 billion in retiree drug subsidies.
- MedPAC says the IRA redesign improves affordability and strengthens plan incentives but also creates new financial pressure and uncertainty for plans.
- The report ties spending acceleration partly to costly medicines such as GLP-1s.
- MedPAC notes that the 2025 national average monthly bid amount rose 180 percent and total expected benefit cost rose 42 percent, larger than many observers expected.
