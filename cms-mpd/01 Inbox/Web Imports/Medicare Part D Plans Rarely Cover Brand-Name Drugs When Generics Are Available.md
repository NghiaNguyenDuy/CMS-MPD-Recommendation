> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- The study examines matched brand-name and generic pairs across Part D formularies from 2012 to 2019.
- In 2019, 84 percent of plan-product combinations had generic-only coverage, while another 15 percent covered both the brand and the generic.
- When both were covered in the same plan, the brand-name drug was placed on a lower cost-sharing tier only about 1 percent of the time.
- The authors conclude that most Part D formularies are designed to encourage generic use, even though some nonpreferred-tier or exclusion patterns for generics still warrant monitoring.
