> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- This cross-sectional study benchmarks Part D coverage in 2019 and 2023 for the first 10 drugs later selected for Medicare drug price negotiation.
- Average plan coverage increased from 82 percent in 2019 to 91.3 percent in 2023.
- Coverage was generally high, but tier placement and utilization management still varied across products.
- The study suggests that formulary inclusion alone does not fully determine beneficiary access because prior authorization, step therapy, and adverse tier placement can still affect use.
