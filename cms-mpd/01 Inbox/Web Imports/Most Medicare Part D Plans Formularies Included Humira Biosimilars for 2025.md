- Humira had an annual list price of about $90,000, and before rebates it cost Part D and enrollees $5.4 billion in 2022.
- OIG found that formulary coverage of Humira biosimilars increased substantially between 2024 and 2025.
- In 2025, 96 percent of PDPs and 88 percent of MA-PD plans covered at least one of the 10 available Humira biosimilars.
- Some plans covered Humira biosimilars but not Humira, but 1 percent of PDP enrollees and 10 percent of MA-PD enrollees remained in plans that covered only Humira.
- OIG also found that almost none of the formularies covering both Humira and biosimilars used preferential tier placement or utilization management to actively encourage biosimilar use.
