> [!note]
> Captured from the URLs package on 2026-04-07.

- OIG examined the 378 unique formularies used by 5,178 Part D plans operating in 2025.
- The review focused on whether those formularies covered 194 of the 200 drugs most commonly used by dual-eligible enrollees.
- OIG found that a majority of 2025 Part D plan formularies covered almost all commonly used drugs and that only a small number of commonly used drugs were not covered by most formularies.
- OIG concluded that dual-eligible enrollees can expect to have access to most drugs in 2025 regardless of the Part D plan in which they are enrolled.
