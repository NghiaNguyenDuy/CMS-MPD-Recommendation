The Medicare Prescription Payment Plan offers Medicare Part D enrollees the option to pay out-of-pocket prescription drug costs in the form of capped monthly payments instead of all at once at the pharmacy. All Medicare prescription drug plans are required to offer this benefit to enrollees.

## Contract Year 2026 Medicare Advantage and Part D Final Rule (CMS-4208-F)

The Contract Year (CY) 2026 Medicare Advantage (MA) and Part D Final Rule codify the requirements established in the final part one and final part two guidance for 2026 and future years of the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan. To review the CY 2026 MA and Part D Final Rule visit the Federal Register .

## Medicare Prescription Payment Plan Part One Guidance

Part one guidance on the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan outlines requirements for Medicare Part D plan sponsors on operational topics such as identifying Medicare Part D enrollees likely to benefit from the program, the opt-in process for Part D enrollees, program participant protections, and the program data collection. These requirements apply to the first year of the program, 2025.

### Communications Resources

Additional public-facing resources on the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan are available on Medicare.gov/publications , where users can easily search for materials by title, key word, or publication number. CMS encourages Medicare Part D plan sponsors to use these CMS-provided resources, such as the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan fact sheet and takeaway card, to satisfy outreach, education, and communications program requirements. Medicare Part D plan sponsors canpublish/print/repurpose the fact sheet and takeaway card in multiple languages to educate Part D enrollees and improve awareness of the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan in multiple languages.
