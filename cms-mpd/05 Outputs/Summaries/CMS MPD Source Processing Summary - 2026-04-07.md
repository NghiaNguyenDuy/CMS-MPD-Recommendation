## Each Source Summary

### Directly Reviewed Markdown Sources

- [Direct text] [[01 Inbox/Articles/Using an Online Decision Aid to Help Medicare Beneficiaries Choose a Prescription Drug Plan|Using an Online Decision Aid to Help Medicare Beneficiaries Choose a Prescription Drug Plan]]: PCORI multi-study project that used focus groups, ethnography, usability testing, and a randomized trial to evaluate the CHOICE tool; expert recommendations increased plan switching and satisfaction, while spending reductions were directionally favorable but not statistically significant.
- [Direct text] [[01 Inbox/Web Imports/Plan Finder Will Play a Key Role in Part D Redesign|Plan Finder Will Play a Key Role in Part D Redesign]]: Avalere interviews with SHIP staff and advocates show that Plan Finder remains central to beneficiary support and should better display redesign-era monthly costs, smoothing, formulary tier changes, saved comparisons, and accessibility features.
- [Direct text] [[01 Inbox/Web Imports/Medicare Part D Risk Adjustments and Bidding Instructions for 2025|Medicare Part D Risk Adjustments and Bidding Instructions for 2025]]: Sponsor-oriented overview of RxHCC and PDE-based projections plus the 2025 bidding calendar, liability shifts, specialty-tier threshold, COB fee, and Medicare Prescription Payment Plan implementation workload.
- [Direct text] [[01 Inbox/Web Imports/What to Know About Medicare Part D|What to Know About Medicare Part D]]: Plain-language NCOA guide for beneficiaries covering why and when to enroll, 2026 average premium and deductible figures, late penalties, creditable coverage, Extra Help, and SHIP support.
